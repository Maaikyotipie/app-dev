# The Last 10 Years (余命10年) 

## About

**The Last 10 Years** (余命10年, *Yomei 10-nen*) is a 2022 Japanese romantic drama film starring **Nana Komatsu** and **Kentaro Sakaguchi**. Based on the bestselling novel by Kosaka Kozue, the film tells a deeply emotional story about love, life, and living with limited time.

## Themes & Analysis

Key themes explored in the film:
-  **Mortality and Time**: Living fully when time is limited
-  **Love and Sacrifice**: The complexities of loving someone with terminal illness
-  **Beauty in Impermanence**: Japanese aesthetic of mono no aware (物の哀れ)
-  **Character Development**: Matsuri's growth from fear to acceptance
-  **Visual Storytelling**: Use of seasons and color palettes to represent emotional states
